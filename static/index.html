How the Hybrid System Intelligently Switches Power

1. Pure Electric Mode

Engine is completely off. The car runs 100% on battery power with zero tailpipe emissions.

2. Series Hybrid Mode

Engine turns on quietly to act as an onboard generator, charging the battery while driving.

3. Parallel Boost Mode

Both engine and electric motor work together for maximum acceleration and uphill climbing.

4. Engine Highway Cruise

Engine directly drives the wheels at high speeds where gasoline engines are most efficient.

5. Regenerative Braking

When slowing down, the motor acts as a generator to recharge the battery using kinetic energy.
